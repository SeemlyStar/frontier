# The character: stress, supplies and rescue

You play one character for the whole game. See the [pillars](../pillars.md).

## Stress

- Stress is the main limit on what you can do. **[Decided]**
- Stress runs from 0 to 100. Hard work, danger, darkness and travel raise it. Resting, sleeping and good company lower it. **[Proposed]**
- Bands: **[Proposed]**
  - **Calm** (under 60): everything is available.
  - **Strained** (60–84): careful work suffers. Observing is less likely to turn up anything new, and reflecting isn't possible.
  - **Overwhelmed** (85–99): only resting, eating, talking and heading back are possible.
  - **Collapse** (100): see [Rescue](#rescue).

## Supplies

- Supplies, not hunger. **[Decided]**
- Supplies are counted in whole units. Routes cost supplies, and so does a night spent away from a bed. Running out while outdoors raises stress quickly. **[Proposed]**
- You can carry at most 6 units. Food and kit are bought at the inn, which restocks a limited amount each day, so supplies can't be hoarded. **[Proposed]**
- Foraging gives a unit but disturbs the location. **[Proposed]**

## Coin

- Coin is earned by helping people with their work, and spent on supplies and a bed. **[Proposed]**
- Coin is deliberately small and capped in what it can buy, so it never becomes the thing that makes the rest of the game trivial (pillar 4). **[Proposed]**

## Rescue

- Danger is low-lethality. Rescue carries real costs. **[Decided]**
- At 100 stress you collapse. Someone finds you: the person who trusts you most, or else the innkeeper. You wake at the inn next morning. **[Proposed]**
- Costs: you lose the rest of the day, half your supplies, and you **owe a favour** to whoever found you. Until you repay it by helping them, they trust you less and others hear about it. **[Proposed]**

## Watch in playtesting

- Is stress tense in the way CK's early game is, without its sudden deaths?
- Does the supply cap stop supplies fading into irrelevance?
