# Investigation and understanding

Investigation is the core loop: understanding the world rather than extracting from it. **[Decided]**

## Facts

- A **fact** is one thing you have noticed or been told. Facts go in your journal. **[Proposed]**
- Facts come from **observing** a place (30 minutes) or from **asking** people. **[Proposed]**
- Observing only turns up a fact when the conditions are right: time of day, the state of the place, or something you already understand. When nothing new is there, observing shows you how the place is right now. **[Proposed]**

## Insights

- An **insight** is understanding formed by connecting facts. You form insights by **reflecting** somewhere quiet, when you hold the facts it needs. **[Proposed]**
- Insights are the main form of progress. They unlock new things to do, routes, and things to talk about. They widen what you notice, rather than making you stronger (pillar 3). **[Proposed]**
- Every insight raises at least one new **question**, with a hint of where to look next (pillar 5). **[Proposed]**
- Some insights give you a choice that changes the world and costs someone something (the late-game "what does this cost, and to whom?" test). **[Proposed]**

## Action taxonomy

- Learn / take / give back / maintain / social-economic, with tend/restore identified as missing. **[Open]**
  - Slice 1 covers learn (observe, ask, reflect), take (forage), social-economic (chat, help) and one give-back choice.

## Watch in playtesting

- Is it clear where to look next without being told?
- Does reflecting feel like your own understanding, or like collecting keys?
