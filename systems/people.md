# People: NPCs with their own lives

People have routines, skills and relationships with each other, not just with you (pillar 2).

## Routines

- Each person has a home, a workplace and a daily schedule that moves them between places. **[Proposed]**
- Their work affects the world: the woodcutter's felling disturbs the woodland, the forager's gathering disturbs the meadow. **[Proposed]**

## Relationships between people

- Every pair of people has an **affinity** from −100 to 100. **[Proposed]**
- When two people are in the same place, they may talk. Talk shifts their affinity, depending on how well their **views** match. Each person holds a view on how humans should live with the land, from "use it" to "leave it be". **[Proposed]**
- Affinity crossing a line makes **news**: a friendship, a quarrel, or a courtship between two people. Quarrelling people avoid each other's evening haunts. **[Proposed]**
- People share what they know when they talk, so knowledge (including things you told them) spreads through the valley. **[Proposed]**

## You and people

- Each person has a **familiarity** (how well they know you) and **trust** in you. **[Proposed]**
- Actions: **[Proposed]**
  - **Chat**: raises familiarity and lowers your stress.
  - **Ask about** a subject: they share something they know, if they know you well enough.
  - **Ask for news**: you hear what has happened between people lately.
  - **Help** with their work: costs time and stress; earns coin and trust, and repays a favour owed.
- Romance with the player, as an optional relationship (Sims-style, no adult content). **[Open]**

## Watch in playtesting

- Do people's relationships change enough to be interesting, without becoming random noise?
- Does news make you want to go and see things for yourself?
