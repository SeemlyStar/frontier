# Slice 1: the valley

A small, living valley below the peak. It is meant to test whether the core loop is fun: a world that moves without you, progress through understanding, and stress and supplies as pressure. **[Decided]** (slice scope)

Everything below is Claude's first pass, built so it can be played and judged. All names, places, people and facts are **[Proposed]** and free to change.

## What it should test

- Do people's lives feel like they carry on without you?
- Does observing, asking and reflecting feel like discovery, and does each insight make you want to know more?
- Do stress and supplies create tension without becoming a chore?
- Does the one consequential choice feel weighty?

## The thread: the valley's lights

The valley glows faintly at night, and lately the glow in the woods has turned amber where trees were felled.

**Facts**
- Reeds at the fords glow pale green when you walk through them at night. (Observe Reed beds at night.)
- Amber light pulses around fresh stumps. (Observe Felling stand when the woodland is disturbed, at night.)
- Ilse: the old folk said amber means hurt, green means all is well. (Ask Ilse about the lights.)
- Tobin: the woodland has grown thick with brambles where he fells; it fights back. (Ask Tobin about the woods.)
- Moths gather at the amber, then fly uphill. (Observe Deep grove at dusk.)
- Garrow: the flock won't graze the high pasture since the amber spread. (Ask Garrow about the slopes.)
- A thin line of light runs up the mountainside toward the peak. (Observe the Lookout at night.)

**Insights**
1. **The lights are a language** (reeds + Ilse). Unlocks *Read the lights*: observing tells you a place's danger exactly, and dangerous travel costs less stress. Question raised: *What is the woodland saying?*
2. **Hurt is carried uphill** (amber + brambles + moths). Reveals the deer trail. Unlocks a choice with Tobin: ask him to cut only deadwood (needs his trust). That calms the woodland, but costs him and Pell their trade, which strains their friendship. Question raised: *Where is the signal going?*
3. **Something above is listening** (insight 2 + flock + line of light). Ends the slice. Question raised: *What at the peak is listening, and what does it do with what it hears?*
