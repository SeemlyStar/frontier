# Setting and tone

## The world

- A low-tech fantasy world. **[Decided]**
- The overarching goal is to reach the highest peak and resolve a mystery about magic and the balance between nature and humanity. **[Decided]**
- Wilderneer shares this setting. The two are separate design efforts; mechanics don't transfer between them unless stated. **[Decided]**

## Light, signals and the world's defence (shared canon with Wilderneer)

- The ecosystem is built on light. Plants and animals emit and read light signals, including danger signals. When a species recognises a threat to itself or to the whole ecosystem, it signals, and other species come to defend. **[Provisional]**
- Magic is the same network as this signalling. Drawing on it risks provoking the world's defence. **[Provisional]**
- At scale, the same defence can treat humans themselves as the threat: the land resists exploitation and becomes harder to live in. It isn't a moral punishment; the world's "will" isn't black and white. **[Provisional]**
- This connection is recorded in Wilderneer's [setting](https://github.com/SeemlyStar/wilderneer/blob/main/setting.md) too. The two should be moved to the shared canon repository once it is set up. **[Open]**

## Tone

- Investigation and understanding over extraction. **[Decided]**
- Low lethality: failure has real costs, but the character doesn't die. **[Decided]**
- Deliberately shallow combat. **[Decided]**
- No single worldview is the game's "correct" one. People hold their own valid views about how humans should live with the land. **[Proposed]** (carried over in spirit from Wilderneer's archetypes)
